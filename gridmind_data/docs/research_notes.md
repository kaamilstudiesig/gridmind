# Research notes

Primary public sources used:
- BEE annual BESCOM energy audit FY2023-24
- BEE annual energy audit index for FY2024-25
- KERC Tariff Order 2025 / BESCOM filing
- OpenCity BESCOM datasets
- Government of India OGD Bengaluru electricity-consumption dataset
- CSTEP feeder survey research

Important boundary:
The public evidence is aggregate/infrastructural. We did not find a public BESCOM dataset providing per-transformer SCADA-style telemetry. GridMind therefore uses synthetic incident telemetry inside a controlled simulator.
